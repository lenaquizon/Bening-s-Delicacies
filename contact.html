Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I place my order?
Because all our delicacies are cooked strictly to order with zero preservatives, we recommend ordering at least 1 to 2 days in advance. For large party orders or holiday trays, please order 3 to 5 days ahead so we can guarantee your preferred delivery slot.
Do you offer delivery or pickup?
We accommodate both! You may pick up your order directly from our home kitchen in Nagcarlan, Laguna, or we can arrange delivery to areas near Liliw, San Pablo, and Santa Cruz (delivery fee depends on your location).
How should I store the delicacies?
Cassava Cake: Best enjoyed warm. Store in the refrigerator for up to 4 days and microwave for 20 seconds before serving.
Espasol: Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days, or refrigerate for up to a week.
Kimchi: Keep refrigerated to maintain crispness and slow down natural fermentation.
Can I bring your delicacies abroad (on flights / as pasalubong)?
Yes! Many of our customers regularly order delicacies to bring abroad as pasalubong for family, friends, and fellow OFWs overseas. Our Espasol bulk boxes (20, 30, and 50 pcs) and sealed Cassava Cake trays travel exceptionally well in carry-on bags or luggage. Let us know your flight departure date and time in your order note so we can time your batch fresh right before you depart for the airport.
