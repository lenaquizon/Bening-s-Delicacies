Pasalubong Across the Miles

Bringing the Taste of Home Abroad

Many of our valued customers order our delicacies specifically to take abroad when leaving the country. Over the years, our freshly rolled Espasol and golden Cassava Cake have journeyed in suitcases and carry-ons across the US, Canada, the Middle East, Europe, and Asia—serving as the ultimate pasalubong for family, friends, and fellow OFWs craving the comfort of home.

Flight & Luggage-Friendly Packaging Packed tightly in durable boxes and sealed containers designed for secure travel in carry-on bags or luggage without shifting or mess.
Timed Fresh for Your Airport Departure Coordinate your schedule with us: we cook and bake your order on the exact morning or evening you depart for NAIA, ensuring maximum shelf life abroad.
100% Authentic Homemade Flavor Zero artificial preservatives or chemicals. Nanay Bening's pure coconut milk recipe tastes just like home, even 8,000 miles away.
